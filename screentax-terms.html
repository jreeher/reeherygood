3. How Charges Work

Screen Tax tracks foreground usage of apps you select and applies a per-hour rate that you configure. Each app may have a free daily allowance (in minutes) before charges begin. The charge formula is:

  • Billable minutes = Total usage minutes − Free allowance minutes (minimum 0)
  • Charge = (Billable minutes ÷ 60) × Hourly rate

Charges accumulate over each calendar month. The completed month's total is processed automatically on the 1st at approximately 6:00 AM local time. If the App cannot run then, the charge is attempted the next time you open it, still for the same month. Charges below the payment processor's minimum transaction amount of $0.50 are skipped for that month and are not carried forward.

Your monthly charge is capped at $25.00 regardless of usage, as a safety limit against misconfiguration or inaccurate usage data.

Uninstalling the App: Charges are initiated by the App on your device. If you uninstall Screen Tax before a month's charge has been processed, that charge will not be collected, and all locally stored data is removed by Android. Charges already processed before uninstalling are not automatically reversed; see Section 6 for refunds.
