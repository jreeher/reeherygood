Screen Tax

Screen time was never free — but now it actually hurts.

A free Android app that charges you for time on your most distracting apps.

Download for Android How billing works

What it is

Screen Tax is a self-imposed accountability tool. You choose the apps that tend to eat your day, decide how many minutes of each are free every day, and set an hourly rate for any time beyond that. The extra time is added to your tab, and once a month your card is charged for it.

You set every rule yourself, so the “tax” is entirely in your control.

1

Pick your apps

Choose which apps count, give each a free daily allowance in minutes, and set an hourly rate.

2

Use your phone

Screen Tax measures how long each tracked app is open. Time inside your daily allowance is always free.

3

Get charged monthly

On the 1st of each month, your card is charged once for the month that just ended.

4

Stay in control

Change your apps, allowances or rates any time. Uninstall the app and future months aren’t charged.

Billing, in plain terms

Free to download. You only pay for time beyond your own allowances.

  • How a charge is figured For each tracked app, each day: minutes used, minus your free allowance (never below zero), divided by 60, times your hourly rate. Days add up over the calendar month.
  • When you’re charged Once a month, on the 1st at about 6:00 AM your local time, for the month that just ended. If the app can’t run at that moment, the charge is attempted the next time you open it — still for the same month.
  • Minimum Months that total less than $0.50 are skipped and are not carried forward.
  • Monthly cap You will never be charged more than $25.00 in a month, no matter how much you use your phone.
  • If a charge fails Screen Tax retries automatically once a day, up to five more times. If every attempt fails, the charge is marked failed and automatic attempts stop. You can still retry it yourself from Settings.
  • If you uninstall Charges are started by the app on your phone. If you uninstall before a month’s charge is processed, that charge is not collected.
Example: an app with 30 free minutes a day and a $2.00 hourly rate. On a day you use it for 90 minutes, 60 minutes are billable (90 − 30), so that day adds $2.00 to the month’s total (60 ÷ 60 × $2.00).

Where the money goes

Charges are paid to Invert LLC, which operates Screen Tax. They’re the fee you agree to pay for going over the limits you set. Stripe’s payment-processing fee is paid by Invert LLC and is never added to your charge.

Charges are not donations: no part of a charge goes to a charity or any other organization, and charges aren’t tax-deductible.

Refunds

Every charge is eligible for a full refund if you ask within 7 days of the charge date. Email admin@invert-able.com with your Customer ID, shown in the app under Settings. Requests after 7 days may still be considered at Invert LLC’s discretion.

Your data

Your per-app screen time never leaves your phone.

  • Stays on your device: the minutes you spend in each app, your app settings, and your charge history.
  • No accounts: there’s no sign-up, email address, or password. Your card is linked to the app on your phone; if you reinstall, you add it again.
  • What our server sees: a token identifying your Stripe customer ID, and the dollar amount of a month at the moment it is charged. Charge records are kept by Stripe.
  • Your card: card details are collected and stored by Stripe. Screen Tax keeps only a Stripe customer ID, the last four digits, and the card brand.
  • Google Firebase is used for crash reports and analytics. Crash reports can include a month’s charge amount.
  • No ads, no selling: we don’t use advertising networks or cross-app tracking, and we don’t sell your data.

To have your saved card and server-side charge records deleted, email admin@invert-able.com with your Customer ID. Read the full Terms of Service and Privacy Policy; both are also shown during setup in the app and are always available under Settings.

Requirements

  • An Android phone running Android 8.0 or newer.
  • The Usage Access permission, which you turn on yourself in Android Settings so the app can measure time in your tracked apps.
  • You must be 18 or older and live in the United States, with a credit or debit card that can be charged in US dollars.

Questions

What counts as screen time?

Only the time a tracked app is open in the foreground, as reported by Android. Apps you don’t choose to track are never charged.

Can I be charged more than $25 in a month?

No. The monthly charge is capped at $25.00 regardless of usage.

What if my month comes to less than $0.50?

It’s skipped — nothing is charged and the amount is not carried into the next month.

Can I change my apps, allowances or rates?

Yes, any time in the app. Changes apply going forward; days that have already ended keep the charge they had when they ended.

How do I get help?

Email admin@invert-able.com for support, refund requests, or data deletion.
