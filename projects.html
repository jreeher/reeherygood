Android App Productivity Free

Screen Tax

Screen Tax is a self-imposed accountability tool that charges you based on how much time you spend on your phone's most distracting apps. You set the rules — choose which apps to track, how many free minutes you get each day, and your hourly rate — so the “tax” is entirely in your control. Taxes are donated to a charity of your choice, turning mindless scrolling into something that actually does good in the world.

Screen time was never free — but now it actually hurts.
